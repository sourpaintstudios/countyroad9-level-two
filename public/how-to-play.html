COUNTY ROAD 9

MUSIC SAVES ALIENS
Turn your sound up. The music is your weapon, and the cows talk back.

The story

It's 1993 on County Road 9, Arizona. Daniel Lee, a guitar-slinging musician, is driving his truck at night when gray aliens in flying saucers come to abduct him. Grays have no souls, which is why they've been messing with cows for 25 years. Daniel's guitar melts them into paint, and the paint becomes a cow with a soul.

You're not killing them. You're saving them.

How to play

  • Tap the sky or hold the dashboard to fire your guitar beam, and slide your thumb to aim. Keep the beam on a ship and the gray melts into a cow: one soul saved. The hold time grows with the waves: 3 seconds in waves 1 and 2, 4 seconds in waves 3 and 4, 5 seconds in wave 5.
  • Leave a ship alone and it dives for your truck. Grabbed? Tap fast to break free.
  • You've got 3 trucks. Lose all 3 and you're abducted: MISSING TIME.
  • Fill the SHRED meter, then tap SHRED! (or press X) for HYPER MODE: a guitar solo that beams every gray on screen, melts them fast, and makes bald cows.
  • Survive 5 waves and save all 8 souls to become a Legend of County Road 9.
  • Need a break? Tap the round pause button under the CR9 mark, or press Esc or P. Reload by accident and your run waits on the title screen for 30 minutes (RESUME RUN).

Scoring

Each gray is 500. Each cow you save is 1,500, multiplied by your streak (up to 5x). Shoot a saved cow by mistake and you lose 1,000.

Tips

  • Don't let go between kills: letting go ends your streak. Pausing does not.
  • Swing onto a diving ship first.
Scoring details
  • Each gray you melt: 500
  • Each saved cow: 1,500 × streak (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, then 5x max). A streak is kills in a row without letting go of the beam.
  • Shooting a saved cow: −1,000 and your streak breaks
  • Point blank (a kill mid-dive, right in your face): 750
  • Fast first kill: 50 × (30 − seconds)

Sign the Witnesses sheet

Save at least one soul (even if you get abducted) and sign the Witnesses of County Road 9 sheet. The sheet stays on screen while you type on the built-in keypad, and your name shows up live in the slot your score earns. Hit SUBMIT and an alien drops your name onto the sheet and kicks the last witness off. Then beat my score.
